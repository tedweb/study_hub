Why

Question 6

The polynomial function P is given by P(x)=(x−3)(x−2)(x+1), and the domain of P is −5 ≤ x ≤ 5. Which of the following is true about the graph of P?

🔒 Explanation locked until you answer

Why

The zeros are x=−1, 2, and 3. A cubic with three distinct real zeros has two local extrema between consecutive zeros. Because the domain is the closed interval [−5,5], absolute extrema exist; here they occur at the endpoints, while the two interior turning points are local but not global. Therefore B.

Question 7

A linear function P is used to model the price, in dollars, of used cars as a function of their age t, in years. It is known that P(4)=7300 and P(7)=5500. Based on this model, which of the following is true?

🔒 Explanation locked until you answer

Why

For a linear model, compute the constant slope: (5500−7300)/(7−4)=−1800/3=−600 dollars per year. So the price decreases by about $600 each year. Choice C.

Question 11

A new special attraction opened at a museum. The museum management tracked the number of people who visited the attraction each day and created a function model M for the number of people for each day d after the attraction opened. Each day they also calculated the rate of change of the number of people visiting the attraction. They created a function model R for the rate of change, in people per day, for each day d after the attraction opened. The function R is given by R(d)=1/200(−d⁴+35d³−411d²+1845d−2686.5). At which of the following values of d does the graph of y=M(d) have a point of inflection?

🔒 Explanation locked until you answer

Why

R is the rate of change of M, so R=M′. Points of inflection of M occur where M′ changes from increasing to decreasing or vice versa—that is, at local extrema of R. The listed extrema occur at about d=3.894, 8.627, and 13.728. Choice D.
